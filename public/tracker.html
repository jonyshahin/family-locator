How to enable location on iPhone:

  1. Open Settings on your iPhone
  2. Scroll down and tap Privacy & Security
  3. Tap Location Services — make sure it's ON
  4. Scroll down and tap Safari (or your browser)
  5. Select While Using the App or Ask Next Time
  6. Come back here and tap the button above
